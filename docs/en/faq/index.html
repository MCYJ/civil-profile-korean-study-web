Frequently asked questions

Are questions English when the listing is English?

No. The website offers Korean and English, while the app UI, questions and explanations are Korean. Translated store copy is separate from the actual learning language.

Is there a subscription or additional unlock?

The planned app is paid once through the store before installation. Included learning does not require a monthly subscription, an additional in-app unlock or a daily free-question allowance. This website currently has no purchase link.

What works offline?

Included questions, explanations, concepts, diagrams, review and local study records work after installation. Purchases, downloads, re-downloads, updates and external official or policy links may need connectivity. Notifications depend on permission and device settings.

Do edits overwrite saved A/B cases?

The new profile workshop keeps current edits and saved A/B cases separate. A slot changes when you explicitly save to it. Loading a different current case does not silently update both saved cases.

Is this official CAD or practical grading?

No. The virtual model joins three stations with two straight segments. It does not provide vertical curves, terrain correction, KS/KDS design compliance or official practical grading.

Can support restore records after deletion?

No account-based or server synchronization recovery service is provided. Check records before deleting the app or changing devices. For issues, send the app version and reproduction steps using the support page.
